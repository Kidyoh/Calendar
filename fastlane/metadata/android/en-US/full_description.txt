Your days, beautifully.

Glass Calendar brings calm pastel task cards, frosted-glass home-screen widgets and a full Ethiopian calendar together in one app.

• Today view with big date, two world clocks and colourful task cards
• Month view with every day laid out by the hour
• Frosted glass and "island" home-screen widgets
• Ethiopian calendar (incl. Pagume) or Gregorian, in English or Amharic
• Ethiopian public holidays, Orthodox feasts and fasts (Abiy Tsom, Nenewe, Hawaryat, Filseta, Nebiyat, Wednesday & Friday fasts), Islamic holidays and monthly saints' days
• Syncs with Google, iCloud, Outlook and any calendar on your phone
• Reminders, smooth animations and haptics
