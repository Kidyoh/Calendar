## Glass Calendar 1.6.1

**Which file?** Most phones: `glass-calendar-1.6.1-arm64-v8a.apk`. Older 32-bit phones: `armeabi-v7a`.

### New
- **Customizable widgets.** Each widget keeps its own calendar (Gregorian, Ethiopian, Islamic or Orthodox), view and style (Glass, Dark or Light).
  - Calendar: Week, Month or Agenda. On the home screen, **Auto** opens into a month when you make the widget taller.
  - Week strip: Week or Month.
  - Progress: Day, Week, Month or Year.
  - Next up: next event or Agenda.
- **Four new widgets:** Date tile, Progress, Next up, Feasts & fasts.
- **Add to home screen.** Customize a widget in the app, tap 📱+, and the home-screen widget comes out exactly the same.
- A customize sheet with a live preview, and an Add-widget sheet with all six types.
- Amharic throughout.

### Fixed
- The sliding highlight on the Week / Month / Agenda switch now shows.
